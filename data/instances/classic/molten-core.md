# Molten Core

## Before you go
- [attune] Attunement to the Core: Lothos Riftwaker (Blackrock Mountain) — needs a Core Fragment from Blackrock Depths. Lets you port in.
- [key] Aqual Quintessence (Duke Hydraxis, Hydraxian Waterlords: Honored) to douse the boss runes; Eternal Quintessence at Revered
- [resist:fire] fire resistance helps on Ragnaros, Magmadar and Gehennas (tanks and melee most)
- [bring] Greater Fire Protection Potions, your class elixirs and food, enough repair gold

## Lucifron
- [dispel:curse] Lucifron's Curse doubles mana and rage costs
- [dispel:magic] Impending Doom on players
- [assign:Mage] decurse the raid
- [dispel:magic] Flamewaker Protectors Dominate Mind a player; dispel it or Polymorph the controlled player

## Magmadar
- [enrage] Frenzy: Tranquilizing Shot it off
- [assign:Hunter] Tranquilizing Shot rotation on Frenzy
- [fear] Panic fears the raid: Tremor Totem, Fear Ward or Berserker Rage
- [move] Lava Bomb: step out of the fire

## Gehennas
- [dispel:curse] Gehennas' Curse cuts healing taken
- [move] Rain of Fire: move out
- two Flamewaker adds need their own tanks

## Garr
- [cc:elemental] eight Firesworn adds: warlocks Banish their assigned one and keep it banished
- [position] Firesworn explode when they die; melee step back
- Antimagic Pulse strips buffs from the raid

## Shazzrah
- [dispel:curse] Shazzrah's Curse increases magic damage taken
- [purge] Deaden Magic on Shazzrah: Purge or Dispel Magic it off
- [position] Blink + Arcane Explosion: he jumps to a random player; stay spread

## Baron Geddon
- [position] Living Bomb: run out of the raid before it explodes
- [move] Inferno: melee get out
- [dispel:magic] Ignite Mana drains mana users

## Golemagg the Incinerator
- [tank] two Core Ragers are tanked away from the boss
- [threat] Magma Splash stacks on the boss tank: swap tanks
- dps the boss only; the dogs die with him

## Sulfuron Harbinger
- [interrupt] four Flamewaker Priests cast Dark Mending: interrupt every heal
- [dispel:magic] their Shadow Word: Pain
- kill the priests before the boss

## Majordomo Executus
- [cc:humanoid] Flamewaker Healers: Polymorph is the usual control (verify)
- [interrupt] Flamewaker Healers' casts
- Magic Reflection: casters stop casting at the adds; Damage Shield: melee stop hitting
- [threat] he teleports a random tank into the fire pit

## Ragnaros
- [position] Wrath of Ragnaros knocks melee back; stand so you don't land in lava
- [resist:fire] Elemental Fire and Lava Splash: tanks want fire resistance
- [tank] keep melee in range or he casts Magma Blast at the raid
- Sons of Flame at submerge: everyone switches to the adds
