# Shadowfang Keep

## Before you go
- [quest] Horde: Deathstalkers in Shadowfang, The Book of Ur, Arugal Must Die (Undercity / the Sepulcher) — pick up before entering
- [bring] poison removal for Fenrus; curse removal makes Silverlaine and Arugal much easier

## Rethilgore
- Plain melee boss; killing him frees the prisoners who open the courtyard door

## Razorclaw the Butcher
- [tank] hits hard but no tricks; tank and spank
- [loot:dagger,agi] Butcher's Slicer (verify)

## Baron Silverlaine
- [dispel:curse] Veil of Shadow: cuts healing taken — remove it from the tank fast
- [loot:ring,str] Silverlaine's Family Seal

## Commander Springvale
- [interrupt] Holy Light: he heals himself
- [dispel:magic] Hammer of Justice stun on the tank
- [loot:shield] Commander's Crest

## Odo the Blindwatcher
- Howling Rage: he hits harder as his health drops — burn the last 30% fast, healers top the tank first
- [loot:staff,spi,heal] Odo's Ley Staff
- [loot:leather,heal] Girdle of the Blindwatcher

## Fenrus the Devourer
- [dispel:poison] Toxic Saliva on the tank
- [loot:leather,ap,sta] Black Wolf Bracers

## Wolf Master Nandos
- [cc:beast] he calls worgs; CC one if the group is struggling (verify they're CC-able)
- [loot:leather,agi,str] Worgpelt Leggings
- [loot:cloak,ap] Wolfmaster Cape
- [loot:cloth,int] Feline Mantle

## Archmage Arugal
- [dispel:curse] Arugal's Curse turns a player into a worgen that attacks the group — remove it fast
- [interrupt] Void Bolt (heavy shadow damage) — interrupt or line-of-sight
- [position] he teleports between ledges; ranged spread out, melee follow him
- [threat] Thunder Shock knocks melee back; tank re-picks him up
- [loot:cloth,heal] Robes of Arugal
- [loot:cloth,int] Belt of Arugal
- [loot:ring,ap,sta] Seal of Sylvanas (Arugal Must Die quest reward)
